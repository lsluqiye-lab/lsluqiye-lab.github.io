About

I work at the intersection of machine learning and financial risk. My focus is on credit risk modeling and fraud detection, with a research thread on how to inject external knowledge — from domain experts, teacher models, or large language models — into lightweight, deployable scoring models.

Much of my work targets the hard, practical regimes of industrial finance: cold start (scarce labels, immature features), temporal distribution shift (models trained on history but scored on the future), and robustness to noisy supervision. I care about methods that are not only accurate but interpretable, stable over time, and cheap to serve in production.

Beyond modeling, I build the agent systems around it — LLM multi-agent harnesses that automate and govern algorithm development, from experimentation and evaluation to deployment — so that domain expertise scales across business lines instead of bottlenecking on any one person.

Publications

When Do External Priors Help Low-Resource Structured Prediction? A Ranking-Level Alignment Study
Preprint
arXiv preprint · cs.LG · Oct 2026
Qiye Lu, Jiang Ji, Liang Zhang
A model-agnostic training objective — Ranking Prior Alignment — that aligns a model's ranking distribution to an external prior (teacher models, LLMs, or domain experts) via a temperature-scaled, annealed KL loss, requiring no external model at inference. Across tree-ensemble and multiple-instance-learning instantiations, we study when such priors genuinely transfer information versus merely act as a regularizer in low-resource, cold-start regimes.
